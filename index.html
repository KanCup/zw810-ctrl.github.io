About

I am a mathematics honors undergraduate at Rutgers University, graduating in May 2027. I transferred to Rutgers in 2025 from Ocean University of China.

I work on the combinatorics around Coxeter groups and Hecke algebras, and on Macdonald-type polynomials. I also formalize mathematics in Lean 4 and contribute to Mathlib.

I am applying to graduate programs for 2027.

Interests
Kazhdan–Lusztig theory, Schubert calculus, geometric representation theory, arithmetic Langlands, formal verification in Lean
